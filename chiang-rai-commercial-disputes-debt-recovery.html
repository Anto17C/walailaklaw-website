CHIANG RAI COMMERCIAL DISPUTES

Commercial Disputes & Debt Recovery in Chiang Rai

Evidence-led strategy for trade, supply, contract and payment disputes.

Focused local advice

Identify the parties, jurisdiction and recoverable evidence

Cross-border and local disputes may involve goods, transport, services, advance payments or several intermediaries. Walailak Law Firm organizes the contracts, delivery and payment evidence and assesses the debtor before recommending demands, negotiation or proceedings.

When clients contact us

  • A supplier or trader remains unpaid
  • Goods, quality or delivery are disputed
  • An advance payment must be recovered
  • An overseas creditor is pursuing a Thai company
  • A guarantor or intermediary is involved
  • Court papers require an urgent response
Scope of assistance

How Walailak Law Firm can assist

The precise scope is confirmed after conflicts, documents, objectives and urgency are reviewed.

Typical work

  • Confirm parties and jurisdiction
  • Organize contracts and delivery evidence
  • Assess claims, defences and time limits
  • Review debtor and asset information
  • Prepare demand and settlement strategy
  • Manage proceedings through our Chiang Rai local counsel when required
A practical point

The commercial counterparty may not be the legal debtor

Orders, invoices, transport records and payments can identify different businesses. The evidence should establish the correct defendant and any guarantee before formal action.
