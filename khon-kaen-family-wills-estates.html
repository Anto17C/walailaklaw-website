Family, Wills & Estates in Khon Kaen

Discreet advice for international families, property owners and overseas beneficiaries.

Focused local advice

Coordinate family status and ownership

International families may have a Thai-foreign marriage, local property, children with more than one nationality and assets overseas. Walailak Law Firm reviews family registrations and ownership before advising on divorce, agreements, wills or estates.

When clients contact us

  • An international divorce
  • Child-related arrangements
  • A cross-border marriage
  • A Thai will
  • Local property or business interests
  • Overseas beneficiaries
