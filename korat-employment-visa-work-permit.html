KORAT EMPLOYMENT & IMMIGRATION

Employment, Visa & Work Permit Support in Korat

Coordinated employment documentation and authorization for Thai businesses and foreign personnel.

Focused local advice

Align the role, employer and authorization

Manufacturers and regional businesses in Korat may employ foreign executives, engineers, technicians or specialists for establishment, training, operations or expansion. Walailak Law Firm reviews the employing entity, proposed duties, qualifications, employment terms and company eligibility before coordinating the appropriate visa and work-permit process.

When clients contact us

  • A foreign executive joining a Korat company
  • An engineer or technical specialist
  • A BOI-promoted company hiring foreign staff
  • A new or renewed work authorization
  • An employment agreement or workplace policy
  • A termination or employee dispute
Scope of assistance

How Walailak Law Firm can assist

The precise scope is confirmed after conflicts, documents, objectives and urgency are reviewed.

Typical work

  • Review company and employee eligibility
  • Confirm role, duties and work location
  • Prepare employment documentation
  • Coordinate visa and work-permit submissions
  • Align BOI and ordinary procedures where relevant
  • Advise on termination or workplace disputes
A practical point

Authorization should match the actual work

The employing entity, job description, workplace and approved permission should remain aligned. Changes in role, employer or location may require review before the employee starts the new arrangement.
