Bar & Entertainment Venue Setup and Licensing in Pattaya

Company structure, licences, premises checks and staffing rules for foreign investors opening or buying a bar, restaurant or entertainment venue in Pattaya and Chonburi.

Venue owners and investors

Get the structure and the licences right before you open

A bar or entertainment venue sits at the crossing of several sets of rules: limits on foreign ownership, local licensing for alcohol and entertainment, zoning and building use, employment and work-permit requirements, and tax. Enforcement against nominee structures has increased, and a venue set up the wrong way can lose its licences or be closed. Walailak Law Firm helps you choose a lawful structure, checks the premises before you sign a lease, guides the licences and permits, and sets up the staffing and contract framework, so the business can operate sustainably. We advise on legal and regulatory compliance. We do not recommend arrangements that disguise foreign ownership.

When clients contact us

  • A foreign investor planning a bar, restaurant or live-music venue in Pattaya or Jomtien
  • A buyer of an existing bar who needs the company, lease, licences and debts checked first
  • A business that wants to add music, DJ events or longer opening hours
  • An owner unsure how to engage managers, bartenders and entertainment staff lawfully
  • An existing venue facing an inspection, a licence renewal or a labour complaint
Scope of advice

What we help with

We cover the main legal areas a venue has to get right.

Ownership and structure

Foreign majority ownership of service businesses is restricted. We explain the lawful options, such as a Foreign Business Licence or another approval, and why nominee shareholders are a legal and enforcement risk.

Premises and zoning

Whether the building and location can lawfully be used as a bar or entertainment venue, and the lease terms that matter.

Licences

Alcohol sales licences, entertainment venue licences, business registrations and, where relevant, music and performance permissions.

Staff and work permits

Work permits for foreign managers, the Thai staffing requirement that usually applies and how to meet it, and employment contracts.

Contractors, commission and social security

Whether staff are employees or genuine independent contractors, commission arrangements, tax and social security.

Inspections and compliance

Preparing for inspections, renewals and complaints, and dealing with the authorities in Pattaya and Chonburi.

Before you commit

Check the premises and the structure before the lease

The most expensive mistakes happen before opening: signing a long lease on premises that cannot be licensed, buying a bar with hidden debts or labour claims, or using a structure that will not survive an inspection. We look at these early so you can renegotiate or walk away.

What we check before you sign

  • The lease term, break clauses and who is responsible for licences
  • The zoning and building-use status of the premises
  • The company, debts, tax and staff records of a bar you are buying
  • Whether your concept is lawful with the licences that are available
  • A realistic timeline and budget for licences and staffing
Support in stages

Choose the stages you need

  1. Structure and feasibility advice: A written recommendation on ownership, capital and approvals.
  2. Premises and lease review: Checks and negotiation points before you commit to a lease.
  3. Company and licences: Company registration, business registration and licence applications.
  4. Staff and work permits: Employment contracts, work permit applications and compliance procedures.
  5. Ongoing support: Renewals, inspections and labour matters after opening.

Each stage has its own scope, agreed with you in writing. You can stop after any stage.
