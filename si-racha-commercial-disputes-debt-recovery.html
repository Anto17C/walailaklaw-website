Commercial Disputes & Debt Recovery in Si Racha

Evidence-led strategy for contract claims, unpaid invoices, supplier disputes and commercial litigation involving Si Racha businesses.

Focused local advice

Assess the contract, evidence and recovery objective early

Disputes around Si Racha’s industrial economy may involve purchase orders, supply contracts, installation work, service failures, unpaid invoices, rejected goods, guarantees or several companies in the same operating group. Walailak Law Firm reviews the claim and the counterparty before recommending negotiation, formal demands, proceedings or enforcement planning.

When clients contact us

  • An unpaid supplier, contractor or service provider
  • A manufacturer disputing quality, delivery or delay
  • An overseas creditor pursuing a Thai company
  • A customer withholding payment or set-off
  • A director facing an urgent summons or filing deadline
  • A dispute involving connected companies or guarantors
Scope of assistance

How Walailak Law Firm can assist

The precise scope is confirmed after conflicts, documents, objectives and urgency are reviewed.

Typical work

  • Review jurisdiction, contracts and dispute clauses
  • Organize invoices, delivery records and communications
  • Assess liability, defences, limitation and counterclaims
  • Conduct corporate or asset checks where appropriate
  • Prepare demands and settlement strategy
  • Manage litigation, hearings and enforcement if required
A practical point

A judgment is not the same as a recovery

The value of proceedings depends on the strength of the evidence, the counterparty’s position, recoverable assets, timing and cost. Those factors should be considered before committing to a recovery route or accepting a settlement proposal.
